1Welcome to the Felmixo Community

Welcome to Felmixo!

Felmixo is a learning and social platform where people can discover, share, and discuss content related to:

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • Technology
  • Science
  • Interesting Facts
  • Educational Knowledge
  • Learning and Innovation

Our goal is to create a positive, respectful, safe, and informative community where everyone can learn and share ideas.

By using Felmixo, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

2Be Respectful to Everyone

Treat all users with respect.

You must not:

  • Harass, threaten, or bully other users.
  • Use abusive or hateful language.
  • Target people based on personal characteristics.
  • Encourage violence or discrimination.
  • Share another person's private information without permission.
  • Intentionally humiliate or intimidate others.

Healthy disagreements and respectful discussions are welcome.

3Share Educational and Useful Content

Felmixo encourages informative, creative, and educational content.

You can share:

  • Interesting facts.
  • AI and technology information.
  • Science-related knowledge.
  • Educational text posts.
  • Long-form educational videos.
  • Knowledge-based quizzes.
  • Community polls.
  • Images that support your content.

Make sure your content is relevant, understandable, and appropriate for the community.

4Prohibited Content

The following content is not allowed:

  • Illegal activities or instructions promoting serious wrongdoing.
  • Threats, harassment, and targeted abuse.
  • Hate speech and discrimination.
  • Sexual exploitation or child sexual abuse material.
  • Graphic violence or encouragement of harm.
  • Scams, phishing, and fraudulent promotions.
  • Malware, malicious links, or harmful software.
  • Copyright-infringing material.
  • Impersonation and identity fraud.
  • Spam and misleading advertisements.
  • Dangerous misinformation that could cause serious harm.
  • Content that violates applicable laws.

Felmixo may remove content that violates these rules.

5Video Guidelines

Felmixo supports long-form videos for educational and informational purposes.

When uploading or sharing videos:

  • Use content you own or have permission to share.
  • Provide an accurate title and description.
  • Do not upload stolen or copyrighted videos without authorization.
  • Do not share harmful or prohibited material.
  • Do not use misleading thumbnails or titles.
  • Do not upload repetitive spam videos.
  • Respect third-party platform rules when sharing external video links.

Unauthorized downloading or rehosting of third-party videos is not permitted.

6Image and Post Guidelines

When sharing images or text posts:

  • Share relevant and appropriate information.
  • Avoid misleading or deceptive claims.
  • Do not publish private information belonging to others.
  • Do not use images without appropriate rights or permission.
  • Avoid repetitive promotional content.
  • Do not post harmful, abusive, or illegal material.

Images should support the topic of the post.

7Quiz Guidelines

Quizzes should encourage learning and knowledge sharing.

Quiz creators must:

  • Use appropriate questions.
  • Provide understandable answer options.
  • Avoid intentionally misleading users.
  • Avoid hateful or abusive questions.
  • Respect copyright when using images and educational material.

Do not manipulate quiz results or use quizzes to harass other users.

8Poll Guidelines

Polls should encourage constructive discussions and opinions.

Users must not:

  • Create polls intended to harass or target individuals.
  • Use polls for threats or discrimination.
  • Manipulate voting results.
  • Create deceptive or fraudulent polls.
  • Use polls to promote illegal activities.

Respect the opinions of other participants.

9Comments and Replies

Comments should contribute to healthy conversations.

You must not:

  • Spam comments.
  • Threaten or insult users.
  • Post malicious links.
  • Share private information.
  • Repeatedly disrupt discussions.
  • Manipulate likes or engagement.

Respectful criticism and constructive feedback are welcome.

10Direct Messages

Use private messaging responsibly.

You must not:

  • Send threats or abusive messages.
  • Harass users repeatedly.
  • Send scams or phishing links.
  • Share inappropriate or illegal content.
  • Impersonate other users.
  • Send unwanted promotional spam.

Users can block and report accounts where these features are available.

11Fake Accounts and Spam

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Creating fake accounts to deceive users.
  • Impersonating individuals or organizations.
  • Buying or selling fake engagement.
  • Using bots to manipulate likes, views, follows, or votes.
  • Repeatedly publishing identical content.
  • Sending unsolicited promotional messages.
  • Attempting to bypass account restrictions.

Felmixo may restrict accounts involved in these activities.

12Copyright and Intellectual Property

Respect the rights of content creators.

Only upload or share content that:

  • You created.
  • You own.
  • You have permission to use.
  • You are legally allowed to distribute.

If you find content that may violate your copyright, report it or contact:

support.felmixoai@gmail.com

Include the content link and relevant ownership information.

13Privacy and Personal Information

Protect your own privacy and respect other people's privacy.

Do not share:

  • Personal phone numbers without permission.
  • Home addresses.
  • Passwords or login credentials.
  • Private conversations without appropriate authorization.
  • Financial or sensitive personal information.
  • Private photographs or documents without permission.

Do not attempt to access another user's account or private information.

14Reporting and Blocking

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these Guidelines:

  1. Use the Report option where available.
  2. Select the appropriate reason.
  3. Provide additional details if requested.
  4. Block the user if necessary.

Felmixo may review reports and take appropriate action.

15Content Moderation and Enforcement

Felmixo may take action when content or accounts violate these Guidelines.

Possible actions include:

  • Removing content.
  • Removing comments or replies.
  • Restricting publishing permissions.
  • Restricting messaging or interactions.
  • Issuing warnings.
  • Temporarily suspending accounts.
  • Permanently banning accounts.

The severity and frequency of violations may influence the action taken.

Serious safety violations may result in immediate restrictions or account termination.

16Administrator and Permission Rules

Felmixo uses an administrator-controlled publishing permission system.

Regular users may create text posts and quizzes by default.

Additional authorization may be required for:

  • Image uploads.
  • Video uploads.
  • External video link posts.
  • Poll creation.

Users must not attempt to bypass these restrictions or misuse administrator functions.

Administrators are responsible for applying platform rules fairly and protecting community safety.

17Educational Accuracy and Responsible Sharing

Felmixo encourages users to share reliable information.

When sharing educational content:

  • Check information before publishing.
  • Avoid presenting opinions as confirmed facts.
  • Correct inaccurate information when discovered.
  • Do not intentionally spread harmful misinformation.
  • Provide sources where appropriate.

User-generated content does not necessarily represent the views of Felmixo.

18Our Commitment

We want Felmixo to be a place where people can:

  • Learn something new.
  • Explore technology and science.
  • Discover interesting facts.
  • Participate in quizzes and polls.
  • Share educational ideas.
  • Connect with other learners.

We appreciate everyone who helps maintain a positive community.

19Updates to These Guidelines

We may update these Community Guidelines as Felmixo grows and new features are introduced.

Updated versions will be published with a revised effective date.

Continued use of Felmixo after applicable updates means you agree to follow the revised Guidelines, subject to applicable law.
