1Introduction

Welcome to Felmixo.

Felmixo is an educational and social content-sharing platform where users can explore Artificial Intelligence, Technology, Science, and Facts through long-form videos, quizzes, polls, and posts.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how content is displayed, how advertisements work, and what choices you have when using Felmixo.

By using Felmixo, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Felmixo, we may collect the following information:

A. Account Information

  • Full name.
  • Username.
  • Email address.
  • Profile picture.
  • Bio and profile information.
  • Google account authentication information.

B. User-Generated Content

When you use Felmixo, you may provide:

  • Text posts.
  • Images.
  • Long-form videos or external video links.
  • Quizzes and quiz questions.
  • Polls and voting responses.
  • Comments and replies.
  • Profile information and other content you choose to share.

C. Social Activity

We may collect information about:

  • Likes and comments.
  • Followers and following.
  • Saved content.
  • Poll participation.
  • Quiz activity and results.
  • Messages and interactions.
  • Reports and blocked accounts.

D. Device and Usage Information

We or our service providers may collect:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • App version.
  • IP address and general technical information.
  • App activity and interaction information.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted by your device settings and applicable law.

3How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide authentication and account security.
  • Display posts, videos, quizzes, and polls.
  • Personalize the For You content feed.
  • Enable likes, comments, follows, and messaging.
  • Send relevant notifications.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Detect spam, fraud, abuse, and inappropriate content.
  • Manage user permissions and administrative functions.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Comply with legal requirements.

4How Content Is Displayed in Felmixo

Felmixo provides different content sections:

For You
Content may be recommended based on interests, activity, interactions, followed creators, content relevance, and recency.
Quiz
Displays educational questions, optional images, answer choices, and quiz results.
Poll
Displays questions, voting options, and poll results where available.
Post
Displays text and supported media shared by users.
Video
Displays long-form educational and informational videos, including supported external video links.

Content visibility may depend on the creator's privacy settings, account status, permissions, moderation decisions, and applicable platform rules.

Felmixo does not guarantee that every user will see identical content or recommendations.

5Content Limits and User Permissions

Felmixo may apply limits and restrictions to protect the platform and its users.

These may include:

  • Restrictions on uploading or publishing certain types of content.
  • Limits on file sizes, video duration, and supported formats.
  • Restrictions on spam, repetitive posts, or abusive activity.
  • Limits on messaging or other activities where necessary.
  • Account-level restrictions imposed for policy violations.

Regular users may create text posts and quizzes according to available app features.

Image uploads, video uploads, external video link posts, and poll creation may require additional permission granted by an authorized administrator.

Felmixo may change content limits and permissions when necessary for security, performance, or platform improvements.

Advertising partners may collect or receive certain information, such as:

  • Approximate location inferred from IP address, where applicable.

This information may be used to:

  • Measure advertising performance.
  • Limit repeated advertisements.
  • Detect invalid advertising activity.

8Public and Private Content

Felmixo may offer different content visibility settings.

Public Content
Content marked public may be visible to other users and may be shared outside the application.
Followers-Only Content
Content marked for followers may be available to eligible followers, subject to account permissions and technical restrictions.

Please remember that no online service can guarantee that content shared with other users will never be copied, captured, or redistributed.

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public posts, comments, or profile fields.

9Direct Messages

Felmixo may provide private messaging between users.

Messages are intended to be accessible to the conversation participants and authorized systems necessary to operate, secure, and moderate the service.

We may process message-related information to:

  • Deliver messages.
  • Maintain messaging functionality.
  • Detect spam and abuse.
  • Investigate reports and security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Do not send passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information through messages.

  • In connection with a business transfer, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not permit third-party service providers to use information beyond their authorized purposes.

  • Administrative access restrictions.
  • Abuse detection and moderation.

12Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

support.felmixoai@gmail.com

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information.
